Street Crime Map UK for iPhone and iPad

What the app does

Street Crime Map UK lets you check any UK postcode or place: street-level crime on a map and in an area report, sold house prices, live flood warnings and flood history, and your local policing team. Save places to get alerts when new data arrives.

Data sources and coverage

Crime and policing: data.police.uk (England, Wales and Northern Ireland). House prices: HM Land Registry Price Paid Data (England and Wales). Floods: Environment Agency (England). Postcodes: postcodes.io. All data is official open data and can be delayed, incomplete or approximate.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas are covered?

Crime and local policing: England, Wales and Northern Ireland (Police Scotland doesn't publish street-level data). House prices: England and Wales. Flood warnings and flood history: England.

Why is the latest month missing?

Police data is usually published 1–2 months after the end of each month, and forces publish at slightly different times. The app shows the latest month that's available and tells you when a month isn't published yet.

Why do crimes appear at the same spot?

Crime locations are snapped to nearby points to protect people's privacy, so several crimes can appear at one point. The map shows them as a group.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro and that you're online.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In the app, go to Settings › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Street Crime Map Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do alerts work?

The app checks the official data services for your saved places in the background and on launch, and sends a notification when there's something new. iOS decides when background checks run, so alerts can arrive at different times.

Does the app work offline?

You can see data you've already viewed, your saved places and flood history while offline. New areas need an internet connection, and the app reloads automatically when you're back online.

Can I use the app in an emergency?

No. In an emergency always call 999. For non-emergency police matters call 101, and for flooding call Floodline on 0345 988 1188.
