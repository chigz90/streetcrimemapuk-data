Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Who we are

Street Crime Map UK is made by Chirag Tailor. Contact: chirag@chiragtailor.co.uk.

What the app does with your data

The app shows official open data about places in the UK. There is no account and no sign-in. We don’t run our own server, and we don’t sell data. If you choose to, the app shares anonymous usage statistics with us – see “Anonymous usage statistics (optional)” below.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used only while you use the app, to show crime, house sales and flood warnings near you. It is sent to the public data services listed below to fetch results. It is not stored by us and is never shared with other users.

Postcodes and places you search

Postcodes, places and coordinates you look up are sent to the public data services below so they can return results. Your saved places stay on your device.

Public data services

data.police.uk (Home Office), HM Land Registry, the Environment Agency and postcodes.io receive the locations and postcodes needed to answer your requests. Apple Maps is used for maps and place search. Each service has its own privacy policy.

Notifications

Alerts are created on your device by checking the public data services for your saved places. No push notification server is used.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We don’t receive your payment details.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

With your permission only, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to tell us how the app is used, so we can improve it. It is off until you choose “Allow” (asked once, when you first use the app), and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage data – this stops collection straight away and deletes the analytics data stored on your device. What’s collected: which screens and features are used (for example “map layer changed” or “filters applied”, with fixed values such as the layer name), app version, device model, iOS version, an app instance ID created by Firebase (not linked to you), approximate country or region that Google derives from your IP address, and in-app purchase and subscription events (product and price). What’s never collected: your location, postcodes, places or searches, addresses, saved place names, your name, email or any other personal details, or your device’s advertising identifier. Google processes this data on our behalf as a data processor; it is kept for 14 months and then deleted. It is not used to track you across other apps or websites, and it is not used for advertising.

Tracking and ads

The app has no advertising, doesn’t use the advertising identifier and doesn’t track you.

Partner links

Insurance comparison links open the partner’s website in an in-app browser. What you do there is covered by their privacy policy.

Your choices

You can turn location and notifications off at any time in iOS Settings, turn anonymous usage statistics off in Settings › Privacy, and delete saved places in the app. Deleting the app removes all its data from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be in the app with a new date.

This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, no tracking and no cookies. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and secure the service – see GitHub's privacy statement.
